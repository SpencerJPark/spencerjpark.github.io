Post-purchase operations & the data architecture underneath

Operations Manager · May 2023 – Oct 2024

Problem
A growing DTC food brand had no fulfillment infrastructure and no way to see its own numbers — orders, costs, and carrier performance lived in disconnected tools, and the true cost of goods drifted invisibly over time.
Approach
Designed the fulfillment facility layout and order-processing workflows, then built the data layer to match: an ETL pipeline (Python, SQL, BigQuery, Spark, Airflow) integrating storefront, order, and fulfillment data — including slowly changing dimensions so historical COGS could be analyzed honestly. Instrumented the digital product with Google Tag Manager and built self-serve KPI reporting for leadership.
Outcome
Leadership went from guessing to reading dashboards. Documented everything in video and writing, with a roadmap of operational changes triggered automatically as scale KPIs were hit — so the playbook outlived my tenure.

Seats EOB
StackPOS · CRM · Inventory · Fulfillment · SaaS evaluation

Consultative discovery for a C-suite that couldn't see its data

Technical Business Consultant (Contract) · Jun 2024 – Oct 2024

Problem
A restaurant group running multiple brands (Just Poke, Matcha Magic) had data scattered across POS, CRM, inventory, and fulfillment systems. Leadership couldn't analyze pricing, costs, or loss — and needed to adopt new SaaS tooling for compliance without knowing what would actually integrate.
Approach
Led discovery with C-suite and operational stakeholders to map business goals, technical architecture, and constraints across brands. Translated those requirements into a scalable data design, unified the system data flows into a governed reporting layer, and evaluated SaaS vendors on technical fit, integration risk, and implementation readiness — before money was spent.
Outcome
Leadership analyzed pricing, cost, and loss across brands for the first time, with a phased implementation roadmap and a clean handoff to internal owners at contract end.
